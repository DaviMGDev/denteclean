# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Assumption from the current site: prospective and returning dental patients who want to understand the clinic's services, meet its team, and request an appointment.

## Product Purpose

The DenteClean website presents the clinic and its dental services, answers common questions, and gives visitors ways to request an appointment or contact the clinic.

## Positioning

The existing site presents DenteClean as a premium dental clinic pairing modern dental care with a welcoming, comfort-focused patient experience. This is the site's stated positioning, not independently verified.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript site includes section navigation, a responsive menu, service and booking dialogs, a contact form with validation and confirmation feedback, and a WhatsApp contact link. Preserve the current Portuguese content, locally stored imagery, and working interactions when changing the presentation.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the current page copy, local clinic, treatment, and team imagery, and a Stitch homepage export at `/tmp/stitch-denteclean-preview/denteclean-stitch-desktop.html`. Existing patient counts, ratings, testimonials, credentials, treatment claims, and contact details are supplied site content and have not been independently verified.

## Product Principles

- Make services, clinic information, and appointment actions easy to find.
- Preserve the content and behavior already present in the site.
- Keep the experience readable and usable on desktop and mobile.
- Do not present supplied claims as independently verified.
